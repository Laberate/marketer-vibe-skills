# Research notes for MarketerVibe Skills V1.0

The MarketerVibe skills are original syntheses, not copies of any one public skill.

## High-signal ecosystem sources studied

- `anthropics/skills` and the Agent Skills specification — portable skill structure and progressive disclosure.
- `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` — product marketing, ad creative, hook system, creative roadmap, short-form video formats, customer-language grounding, and marketing psychology.
- `ComposioHQ/awesome-claude-skills` — broad ecosystem discovery.
- `alirezarezvani/claude-skills` — cross-agent marketing skill patterns and portability.
- `openai/skills` image-generation material — explicit reference roles, invariants, compositing, photorealistic-natural prompting, and deliberate iteration.

## Narrow domain sources studied

- `whatsuppiyush/ad-maker-claude-skill` — angle x awareness thinking and diversified hook families.
- `SupercmoHQ/superCMO-skills` — product-first campaign planning, competitor-ad research, UGC/video scripting, product analysis, and product-photo fidelity practices.
- `hlyboki-sensy/photosession` — material-light coupling and QA thinking.
- `cheney-yan/photorealistic-skill` — caught-moment realism, skin/light/focus cues; identity-altering invented-imperfection logic was intentionally excluded.
- `beatra-ai/ecommerce-product-images-skill` and `beatra-ai/ai-product-photography-skill` — confirmed SKU facts, scale/fit facts, and product-fidelity workflow patterns.
- `motiful/product-shots` — e-commerce product-visual workflow patterns.

## MarketerVibe-original core retained

- physical-size authority hierarchy for human/product UGC
- reference size firewall, including Session Master losing size authority when real dimensions exist
- state-specific measurement scope
- hand-adapts-to-product doctrine
- two-hand human feasibility logic
- secondary packaging may leave frame when action realism requires it
- Session Master as continuity root, not pose template
- compact image-to-video handoff
- strategy-before-images / exact-script-after-images architecture
- strongest truthful angle that the approved visuals can actually execute
- two-mode UGC strategy skill: pre-image strategy + post-image execution
